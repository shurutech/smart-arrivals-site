Last updated: September 4, 2026

Program Description

Smart Arrival LLC ("Smart Arrivals," "we," "us," or "our") offers an SMS notification program ("Program") that sends patients a courtesy text message when a clinic is running behind schedule, letting you know an updated arrival time. This Program does not change, cancel, or reschedule your appointment. Any change to your official appointment must be made by calling your clinic directly.

How You Are Enrolled

You are enrolled in this Program by providing your mobile phone number and consent during your clinic's standard patient intake process (in person or by phone). By providing your number and consenting, you agree to receive automated SMS text messages from Smart Arrivals related to your appointment arrival time.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your appointment and how the clinic day is running. You should expect to receive up to 3 messages per appointment day.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out

You can opt out of this Program at any time. Text STOP to the number you received messages from to stop receiving all future SMS messages from Smart Arrivals. You will receive a one-time confirmation message that you have been unsubscribed. After that, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us unless you opt back in.

Help

For help, text HELP to the number you received messages from, or contact us directly at dylanmoon1229@gmail.com. Standard message and data rates apply to any HELP message sent.

Supported Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Participating carriers include major U.S. wireless carriers.

Privacy

We do not share, sell, or rent your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your phone number is used solely to operate this Program. See our Privacy Policy for more detail on how we handle your information.
