The Waiting Room Problem

High-volume practices face a daily challenge that costs patients, staff, and revenue.

Delays Compound

Small delays around each visit build up throughout the day, pushing the schedule further behind.

Patients Wait for Hours

Patients arrive on time and end up waiting in a crowded lobby, growing frustrated.

Walkouts Cost Revenue

Frustrated patients walk out. That's lost revenue and damaged relationships.

Manual Solutions Fail

Staff are too busy to monitor delays. By the time anyone notices, it's too late.

The Smart Arrivals Solution

Continuous monitoring and automated patient communication, so your waiting room stays manageable.

Real-Time Monitoring

Continuously tracks your clinic's schedule and knows exactly when you're running behind.

Automated Messaging

Patients receive updated arrival times via text—no staff action required.

Intelligent Timing

Acts only when needed, with smart limits to avoid overwhelming patients with messages.

Configurable Rules

Owner Dashboard

Staff Notifications

How It Works

Intelligent automation that runs in the background all day

1

Connect

Smart Arrivals integrates with your existing practice management system using secure, read-only access.

2

Monitor

The system continuously watches your schedule and intelligently determines when the clinic is running behind.

3

Notify

Patients who haven't left home yet receive a text with an updated arrival time—automatically.

4

Adapt

As conditions change throughout the day, the system adjusts. Patients always have a current arrival time.
